🏔️ Top Tourist Destinations in India

Goa – Beaches & Nightlife

Golden beaches, vibrant nightlife, water sports, and Portuguese heritage make Goa a perfect destination for relaxation and fun.

Jaipur – The Royal Pink City

Jaipur is famous for its majestic forts, royal palaces, and rich Rajasthani culture. A must-visit for history lovers.

Kerala – God’s Own Country

Experience peaceful backwaters, lush greenery, ayurvedic wellness, and traditional houseboat stays.

Manali – Snow & Adventure

Manali offers breathtaking mountain views, snowfall, trekking, and adventure sports for thrill seekers.

Varanasi – Spiritual Capital of India

One of the oldest living cities in the world, Varanasi is known for its ghats, temples, and the sacred Ganga Aarti.

📅 Best Time to Visit India

The ideal time to explore most parts of India is November to February, when the climate is pleasant and perfect for sightseeing.
